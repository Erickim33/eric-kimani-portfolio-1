About Me

I learn by building things that work.

I'm a self-taught developer with a background in teaching, writing, and technology. I became interested in software development because I wanted to understand how ideas could become products that people can actually use.

Over time, I've worked across frontend and backend development, mobile applications, databases, authentication, and deployment. My approach is practical: I learn a technology, apply it to a real problem, and keep improving the product as I discover what works and what doesn't.

I'm particularly interested in building applications with a strong user experience and a solid technical foundation. Whether I'm developing a card game or a messaging platform, I enjoy working through the details that turn an idea into something functional.

I'm still growing as a developer, but I'm no longer just learning about software development. I'm building it.
